================================================================================
  AYURBALANCE — VALUE WE PROVIDE & HOW TO DO BETTER
================================================================================

This document summarizes what value we currently give users, where we fall short,
and concrete ideas to add more value and make the product better.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHO ARE OUR USERS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Primary: Dietitians, Ayurvedic practitioners, wellness coaches.
  Secondary: Individuals who want to understand Ayurvedic diet and manage
  their own diet/wellness with a dosha lens.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. VALUE WE ARE ALREADY PROVIDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ✓ Patient management
    - Create and store patient profiles (name, age, gender, dosha, diet notes,
      bowel movements, water intake, allergies). One place to see all patients
      and last visit.
    - Value: Saves time, keeps records in one place, supports continuity of care.

  ✓ AI-powered Ayurvedic diet plans
    - Generate personalized diet plans from free-text patient data (dosha,
      imbalances, preferences). Edit the plan and save it to a patient.
    - Value: Speeds up plan creation, brings consistency with Ayurvedic framing,
      reduces manual writing.

  ✓ Recipe analysis (AI)
    - Paste recipe name, ingredients, and instructions; get Ayurvedic and
      nutritional breakdown (dosha effects, qualities, suggestions).
    - Value: Helps practitioners and users judge if a recipe fits a patient
      or their own constitution; educational.

  ✓ Ayurvedic Q&A (AI chat)
    - Ask questions in plain language about diet, wellness, doshas; get
      structured answers.
    - Value: Quick reference, learning, and support for client questions
      without digging through books.

  ✓ Food database
    - Searchable list of foods with category, calories, Ayurvedic properties
      (e.g. V-P-K+), qualities. Click a food for AI-generated detailed
      analysis.
    - Value: Fast lookup for “what’s good for Vata?” type questions; supports
      diet planning and patient education. Setup page lets you seed Firestore
      from bundled food data.

  ✓ Profile & identity
    - User profile with display name, photo (upload or webcam), and a snapshot
      of patient counts. Links to classic texts (Charaka Samhita, etc.).
    - Value: Personalization and a sense of “my workspace.”

  ✓ Settings
    - Dark/light theme, notification toggle.
    - Value: Comfort and control over the app experience.

  ✓ Export
    - Diet plan tool can export the plan as PDF and save plan to patient.
    - Value: Shareable with patients, printable, attached to their record.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  2. GAPS — WHERE WE’RE NOT DELIVERING ENOUGH VALUE YET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Dashboard “Explore” links were wrong
    - Fixed: Recipe Analysis → /recipe-analysis, Q&A → /q-and-a, Wellness
      Journey → /profile. (Previously pointed to /public/... and wrong paths.)

  • “Patient Wellness Journey” is underused
    - Profile shows patient list and counts but no real “journey” (e.g. over
      time charts, dosha balance trends, before/after notes). The value
      promise is higher than what’s there.

  • Notifications are UI-only
    - Toggle exists but doesn’t trigger real notifications (browser push,
      email, or in-app). So we’re not yet delivering on “receive alerts and
      updates.”

  • No real “recommend foods for this patient” flow
    - There’s a flow file (recommend-foods-for-patient) but it’s not clearly
      wired in the UI. Food DB and diet plan are separate; “suggest foods for
      this dosha/imbalance” from the patient page would add clear value.

  • Food database depends on Firestore
    - If “foods” collection is empty, the page is empty. Setup exists but
      users must run it once and know about /setup. First-time experience can
      feel broken without a nudge or onboarding.

  • Limited onboarding
    - New users land on dashboard without guidance (e.g. “Add your first
      patient,” “Run Setup to load foods,” “Try Recipe Analysis”). Power users
      are fine; new users may not see the value quickly.

  • No reminders / follow-ups
    - No “next visit” or “follow-up due” for patients. Practitioners have to
      remember or use another system.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  3. WHAT WE CAN ADD TO PROVIDE MORE VALUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  High impact, feasible

  • Wire “Recommend foods for patient” into the UI
    - From a patient’s profile or from the diet-plan tool: “Suggest foods for
      this patient” using existing recommend-foods-for-patient flow. Show list
      (and optionally add to diet plan). Clear, practical value.

  • Real “Wellness Journey” on Profile (or dedicated page)
    - Per-patient: timeline of visits, diet plan versions, optional notes.
    - Simple charts: e.g. “number of plans over time” or “dosha focus over
      time” if we store that. Makes the “Patient Wellness Journey” card
      honest and useful.

  • First-run checklist / onboarding
    - After signup: “1. Run Setup to load food database” → “2. Add your first
      patient” → “3. Generate a diet plan” or “Try Recipe Analysis.” One-time
      checklist in dashboard or modal. Reduces “empty app” confusion.

  • Dashboard empty states and signposting
    - If no patients: “Add your first patient” with direct link and short
      benefit. If foods collection empty: “Load food database (Setup).” Makes
      next step obvious.

  • Next visit / follow-up date on patients
    - Optional “next visit” or “follow-up due” date on patient. List or
      dashboard widget: “Follow-ups this week.” Reminders can come later
      (email/push) but even a visible list adds value.

  Medium impact

  • Real notifications
    - Use browser push (and/or email) for: follow-up due, diet plan saved,
      or “weekly summary.” Tie to Settings → Notifications so the toggle
      matters.

  • Smarter diet plan defaults from selected patient
    - In diet plan tool, when user picks a patient, pre-fill “patient data”
      from that patient’s record (dosha, notes, etc.) so they only tweak
      instead of retyping.

  • Export patient summary (PDF)
    - One-click “Patient summary” PDF: demographics, last plan snippet, key
      notes, next visit. Good for sharing with the patient or another
      practitioner.

  • Recipe save / library
    - Save analyzed recipes (name + analysis) to a “My recipes” or attach to
      a patient. Reuse and build a practice-specific recipe bank.

  Nice to have

  • Multi-language (e.g. Hindi, regional) for key terms or full UI.
  • Mobile-friendly layout improvements so it’s comfortable on tablets/phones.
  • Dosha quiz for end-users (patients) to suggest dominant dosha; practitioner
    can confirm or adjust in patient profile.
  • Integrations: calendar (for next visit), or export to other tools.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4. WHAT WE CAN MAKE BETTER (IMPROVE EXISTING FEATURES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Diet plan tool
    - Pre-fill from selected patient (see above).
    - Optional “meal timing” or “avoid these foods” from patient allergies
      auto-injected into the prompt so the AI never suggests allergens.
    - Clearer “Save to patient” success state and link to open that patient.

  • Recipe analysis
    - “Best for dosha” one-liner at top (e.g. “Best for: Kapha; use in
      moderation for Pitta”).
    - Optional “Save this recipe” to library or to a patient.

  • Food database
    - Filter by dosha (e.g. “Good for Vata”), category, or quality. Right now
      search is by name; filters would speed up “what can I recommend?”
    - After Setup, show a short success message and link to Food database.

  • Q&A
    - Suggested questions (e.g. “What foods balance Vata?” “Is yogurt good for
      Pitta?”) for new users.
    - Optional “Copy” button on answers for quick paste into notes or plans.

  • Patients list
    - Filter by dosha or “has diet plan” / “no plan yet.”
    - Sort by last visit, name, or next follow-up (once we add that field).
    - Bulk export (CSV/PDF) of patient list for admin or reporting.

  • Profile
    - Turn “Patient Wellness Journey” into real per-patient journey (or move
      that card to a dedicated “Reports” or “Journey” page that shows trends).
    - Optional “practice name” or “title” so PDFs and exports look more
      professional.

  • Auth & onboarding
    - After first login, one-time modal or banner: “Load food database (Setup)
      to use Food DB and AI food analysis.” Reduces confusion when Food DB is
      empty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  5. SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  We already provide real value: patient records, AI diet plans, recipe
  analysis, Q&A, and a searchable food database with AI deep-dive. The main
  gaps are: (1) “Wellness Journey” overpromises vs what’s there, (2)
  notifications are not real yet, (3) “recommend foods for patient” is not
  visible in the UI, (4) first-time and empty-state experience can be
  improved.

  Quick wins that add the most value for effort: fix dashboard links (done),
  add “Recommend foods for this patient” to the UI, add next-visit/follow-up
  and a simple “follow-ups this week” list, pre-fill diet plan from selected
  patient, and add a short first-run checklist or empty-state nudges. Then
  improve “Wellness Journey” with real data and, when possible, real
  notifications.
